PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

What it is
PM-KISAN is a Central Sector scheme, fully funded by the Government of India, that became
operational on 1 December 2018. It provides direct income support to eligible land-holding
farmer families across the country.

Benefit amount
Eligible families receive Rs. 6,000 per year, paid in three equal installments of Rs. 2,000
each, credited directly into the beneficiary's bank account.

Eligibility
The scheme covers land-holding farmer families. For this scheme, "family" is defined as
husband, wife, and their minor children. State governments and union territory administrations
are responsible for identifying eligible families based on the scheme guidelines and land
records.

Who is excluded
The following categories are not eligible for PM-KISAN benefits, even if they hold agricultural
land:
- Institutional land holders.
- Former or current holders of constitutional posts, ministers, or members of Parliament,
  state legislatures, municipal corporations, or district panchayats.
- Serving or retired officers and employees of the central or state government and its
  departments, public sector undertakings, and autonomous institutions (Group D / Class IV and
  Multi-Tasking Staff employees are exempt from this exclusion, i.e. they remain eligible).
- Retired pensioners whose monthly pension is Rs. 10,000 or more (excluding Group D / Class IV
  pensioners).
- Any person who paid income tax in the last assessment year.
- Professionals such as doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered accountants, and architects who
  are registered with their professional bodies and practising.

eKYC requirement
Biometric-based eKYC is mandatory for every registered farmer to continue receiving
installments. It can be completed through Common Service Centres (CSCs), the PM-KISAN mobile
app, or the official portal's eKYC option.

How to apply
Farmers can register as new beneficiaries through the official PM-KISAN portal, at Common
Service Centres (CSCs), through eMitra kiosks (in some states), or via state government-
authorised local revenue officials.

Source: pmkisan.gov.in (official PM-KISAN portal, Government of India)
