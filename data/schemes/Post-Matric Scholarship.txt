Post-Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Caste (SC) Students

What it is
The Post-Matric Scholarship (PMS) scheme for SC students is a centrally sponsored scheme run by
the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India, implemented through state
governments and union territory administrations. Its objective is to provide financial
assistance to Scheduled Caste students studying at the post-matriculation level, to support
their enrolment and continuation in higher education.

Eligibility
- The applicant must belong to a Scheduled Caste (SC).
- The applicant must be enrolled in a post-matriculation or post-secondary course, which
  includes general, technical, and professional courses (from Class 11 up to postgraduate and
  research-level studies).
- The annual income of the student's parents or guardian must not exceed the prescribed income
  ceiling. Under the scheme as it has stood in recent years, this ceiling is Rs. 2.5 lakh per
  year; the government has proposed raising this ceiling to Rs. 4.5 lakh in upcoming scheme
  revisions, so applicants should check the current year's official notification.
- An income certificate from a competent authority is required to prove eligibility under the
  income ceiling.

Benefit amount
The scholarship covers:
- Compulsory non-refundable fees charged by the institution (tuition and other admissible fees),
  reimbursed to the student.
- A monthly maintenance allowance, which varies by course level and hosteller/day-scholar
  status. For hostellers, the maintenance allowance has ranged from about Rs. 380 to Rs. 1,200
  per month; for day scholars, it has ranged from about Rs. 230 to Rs. 550 per month, depending
  on the course level (post-matric up to postgraduate).

How to apply
Applications are submitted through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) at scholarships.gov.in.
Some states also run their own state-specific SC welfare portals in addition to, or instead of,
the NSP; students should check which portal applies to their state. Scholarship amounts, once
approved, are paid directly to the student's bank account through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

Note on state variation
While the core scheme is defined at the national level by the Department of Social Justice and
Empowerment, individual states may set their own application windows, additional document
requirements, and in some cases their own income ceilings under state-run SC welfare
scholarships. Students should confirm the exact rules for their state before applying.

Source: scholarships.gov.in (National Scholarship Portal, Department of Social Justice and
Empowerment, Government of India) and haryanascbc.gov.in / sewa.haryana.gov.in (Government of
Haryana), for state-level implementation details.
