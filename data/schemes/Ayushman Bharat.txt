Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB PM-JAY)

What it is
Ayushman Bharat is a flagship scheme of the Government of India, launched following the
recommendations of the National Health Policy 2017, with the goal of achieving Universal Health
Coverage. It has two components: Health and Wellness Centres for primary care, and Pradhan
Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY), which provides hospitalization insurance cover for secondary
and tertiary care.

Coverage amount
PM-JAY provides a cashless health cover of up to Rs. 5,00,000 (5 lakh) per eligible family, per
year, for listed secondary and tertiary care hospitalizations. Any treatment cost above this
amount must be paid by the beneficiary. Coverage is on a family floater basis, meaning there is
no cap on family size or the age of family members covered.

What is covered
Benefits include medical consultation, pre-hospitalization expenses, medicines, diagnostic
tests, medical implants where required, accommodation, food during the hospital stay, intensive
care, treatment of complications arising during care, and post-hospitalization follow-up care
for up to 15 days after discharge. The scheme covers 874 treatment packages and 1,592
procedures across secondary and tertiary care. Pre-existing diseases are covered from day one,
with no waiting period. Outpatient department (OPD) consultations are not covered.

Eligibility
Beneficiary families are identified from the Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) 2011 database,
based on defined deprivation and occupational categories (for example, in rural areas, families
in specified deprivation categories; in urban areas, occupational categories such as domestic
workers, street vendors, and construction workers). There is no income criterion for families
already identified under SECC-2011. Separately, from 29 October 2024, the scheme was extended to
cover all citizens aged 70 years and above, regardless of their income or socio-economic status.
Some states also extend eligibility to additional categories under state-specific schemes with
their own income limits.

How to get an Ayushman card
Eligible individuals can get their Ayushman card made at Ayushman Kiosks located in empanelled
hospitals or at Common Service Centres (CSCs). Eligibility can be checked online at
mera.pmjay.gov.in.

Documents required
A PM/CM entitlement letter (where applicable), ration card, or Aadhaar card is used for
identification when applying for or availing benefits under the scheme.

Where treatment is available
Treatment is cashless at empanelled government hospitals, Community Health Centres (CHCs),
Primary Health Centres (PHCs), and empanelled private hospitals. The scheme has national
portability, meaning an eligible beneficiary can avail treatment at any empanelled hospital
anywhere in India, not only in their home state.

Support and grievances
Beneficiaries can call the toll-free helpline numbers 14555 or 1800-111-565 for assistance.
Grievances can be filed at cgrms.pmjay.gov.in.

Source: nha.gov.in (National Health Authority) and ayushmanbharat.haryana.gov.in
(Government of Haryana), official government portals for the AB PM-JAY scheme.
