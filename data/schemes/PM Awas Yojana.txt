Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Urban (PMAY-U)

What it is
Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Urban (PMAY-U) is a flagship mission of the Government of India,
implemented by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA), launched on 25 June 2015. Its
goal is to address the urban housing shortage by ensuring a pucca (permanent) house for all
eligible urban households, including slum dwellers.

Target groups and income categories
The scheme targets four income categories:
- EWS (Economically Weaker Section): annual household income up to Rs. 3.00 lakh.
- LIG (Low Income Group): annual household income between Rs. 3.00 lakh and Rs. 6.00 lakh.
- MIG-I (Middle Income Group I): annual household income between Rs. 6.00 lakh and Rs. 12.00
  lakh.
- MIG-II (Middle Income Group II): annual household income between Rs. 12.00 lakh and Rs. 18.00
  lakh.

Implementation options
The mission offers beneficiaries assistance through four verticals: In-Situ Slum Redevelopment,
Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS), Affordable Housing in Partnership, and Beneficiary-Led
Individual House Construction/Enhancement.

Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS) - interest subsidy amounts
Under CLSS, an interest subsidy is credited upfront to the beneficiary's home loan account,
reducing the effective loan amount and the EMI. The maximum interest subsidy amounts are:
- EWS and LIG: up to Rs. 2,67,280, on loan amounts up to Rs. 6 lakh.
- MIG-I: up to Rs. 2,35,068, on loan amounts up to Rs. 9 lakh.
- MIG-II: up to Rs. 2,30,156, on loan amounts up to Rs. 12 lakh.
Any loan amount taken beyond these caps is charged at normal, non-subsidized interest rates.

House size standards
For EWS beneficiaries, the constructed house can be up to 30 square metres of carpet area,
though states and union territories have the flexibility to increase this size limit with the
Ministry's approval.

Gender provision
Under the mission's guidelines, the female head of the household is required to be the owner or
co-owner of the house allotted under the scheme.

Scale
The scheme was designed to address a validated housing demand of approximately 1.12 crore
(11.2 million) eligible urban families.

How to apply
Eligible beneficiaries can apply online through the official PMAY-Urban portal, or through Common
Service Centres (CSCs) and urban local bodies (municipalities), depending on the implementation
vertical chosen.

Source: pmaymis.gov.in and pmay-urban.gov.in (Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs, Government
of India), official portals for the PMAY-Urban scheme.
